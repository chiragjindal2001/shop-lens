# Shipping Policy

## Where we deliver
Shoplens delivers to more than 19,000 pin codes across India, including all metro cities and most towns, such as Panipat, Karnal, Jaipur, Pune and Coimbatore. Enter your pin code on the product page or at checkout to confirm delivery to your address. We do not ship outside India.

## Delivery times
Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) usually receive orders in 2 to 4 business days. Other cities and towns take 4 to 7 business days. Remote areas can take up to 10 business days. Delivery estimates are shown at checkout and in your order details.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders of ₹499 and above. For orders below ₹499 a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies. Express delivery is not available at this time.

## Tracking your order
Once your order ships we send an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also see the current status and expected delivery date under Orders in your account, or ask our support assistant "where is my order".

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the package is returned to us and the amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the package reaching our warehouse. Please make sure your phone number and address are correct.
