# Payments Policy

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), net banking from major Indian banks, popular wallets, and cash on delivery. EMI is available on selected cards for orders above ₹3,000.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery (COD) is available on orders up to ₹20,000 in serviceable pin codes. A COD handling fee of ₹30 applies. Please keep the exact amount ready. COD is not available for some high-value items; this is shown at checkout.

## Payment failed but money deducted
Sometimes the bank deducts the amount even though the payment shows as failed. In most cases the amount is automatically reversed to your account within 5 to 7 business days. If the money has not come back after 7 business days, or an order was not created, contact support so that we can raise a ticket with the payment gateway.

## Security
We never ask for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or OTP over chat, phone or email. Please do not share these with anyone, including our support assistant. Payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant gateways, and we do not store your full card details.

## Invoices and GST
A GST invoice is emailed once your order ships. You can add your GSTIN at checkout to get a business invoice. Invoices cannot be changed after the order is delivered.
