# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 10 days of delivery. The window starts on the day the order is delivered, not the day it was placed. Orders delivered more than 10 days ago are not eligible for a return, but you can still contact support if the item is faulty.

## What can be returned
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories, manuals and tags. Earphones and earbuds can be returned only if the seal or hygiene film is intact, unless the item is defective. Software, gift cards and items marked non-returnable on the product page cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Tell our support assistant which order you want to return and why. If the order is eligible, we create a return request and arrange a pickup from your delivery address. Pickup is usually scheduled within 2 to 3 business days. Keep the item packed and ready.

## Refunds
After the returned item passes our quality check, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Cards, UPI and net banking refunds take 5 to 7 business days to show up. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to the bank account or UPI ID you provide. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

## Replacements and exchanges
If an item arrives defective or damaged, we can replace it instead of refunding, subject to stock. Report the problem within 48 hours of delivery with photos.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for a full refund while its status is Placed or Packed. Once an order has Shipped it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse delivery or return it after delivery under the return policy above.
