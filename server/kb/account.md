# Account Help

## Changing your delivery address
Open Account, then Addresses, and edit or add an address. The default address is used for new orders. An address cannot be changed on an order that has already shipped. For orders that are not yet shipped, contact support as soon as possible.

## Updating your name, email or phone number
Open Account, then Profile, and update your details. A changed phone number or email must be verified with a one-time code before it takes effect.

## Resetting your password
On the login page choose Forgot password and enter your email. If the email belongs to an account, a reset link is sent that stays valid for 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder.

## Deleting your account
To delete your account and personal data, contact support with the request. Open orders must be delivered or cancelled first. Some order and invoice records are kept as required by Indian tax law.

## Talking to a human
Ask the support assistant to speak to someone and it will create a support ticket with a ticket number. A human agent replies by email, usually within 1 business day.
