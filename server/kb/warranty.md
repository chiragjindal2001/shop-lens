# Warranty Policy

## Warranty coverage
Most electronics sold on Shoplens carry the manufacturer's warranty, typically 1 year from the date of purchase for headphones, earbuds, smartwatches, power banks and computer accessories. Cables, chargers and other small accessories usually carry 6 months. The warranty period for each product is listed on its product page.

## What the warranty covers
Warranty covers manufacturing defects and faults that occur under normal use. It does not cover physical or liquid damage, damage from unauthorised repairs, normal wear and tear such as worn ear tips, or loss and theft.

## How to claim warranty
Keep your invoice; it is your proof of purchase. For a warranty claim, contact our support assistant or the manufacturer's service centre listed in the warranty card. Many brands offer a repair, replacement or service-centre visit; the exact process depends on the brand.

## Damaged or defective on arrival
If an item arrives damaged or does not work, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos or a short video of the package and the item. We will arrange a replacement or a refund. Damaged items are escalated to a human support agent.
